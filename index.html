FAQ

Questions for HR.

What is my intern, exactly?
An AI agent that looks after one Solana wallet. It reads every token you hold, checks the bag against your rules, drafts trades between crypto, cash and tokenized stocks, and writes you a report. You can also chat with it.
Can it trade without me?
No. It only prepares trades. Each one opens in your own wallet (Phantom, Backpack or Solflare) and nothing happens unless you approve it there.
How does it get promoted?
By trades you approve and how they perform: Analyst after your first approved trade, Associate after 3 with results at or above zero, VP after 6 at +2% or better, Partner after 10 at +5% or better. Each rank can move a bigger share of the bag at once.
What are tokenized stocks?
Tokens on Solana that track real shares such as NVIDIA or the S&P 500 (for example NVDAx or SPYx). Your intern can hold them next to your crypto. Check the issuer's terms and whether they're available where you live.
Is INTERN part of Backpack?
No. INTERN is an independent project. It works with the Backpack wallet (and Phantom and Solflare), and it trades the same kinds of spot markets Backpack Learn covers: tokenized US stocks, BTC, ETH and SOL, all on Solana.
Where does my intern's data live?
In your own browser. Its name, rules and trade history are saved on your device, and your wallet balances are read live from Solana each time.
Is this financial advice?
No. Your intern is software that follows your rules. Crypto and tokenized stocks can lose value quickly. Only use what you can afford to lose.
